## Trained adapter comparison: job 29974

The 200-step pilot (job 29967) selected step 200 on validation loss: 0.5087598581
before training versus 0.2877913692 afterward (43.43% lower). This is a denoising
objective result, not a perceptual improvement percentage.

Copied outputs/lora-prompts-29974 confirms completed generation with the trained adapter.
Local inspection verified all 12 base/adapter listening WAV hashes and five-second,
44.1 kHz stereo 16-bit formats. Model revision, scheduler configuration, inference steps,
guidance, prompts and generator seeds match across paired samples. All six adapter WAVs
differ from their base counterparts. None contains full-scale PCM samples; this check
cannot exclude distortion already present upstream.

User listening assessment: all pairs sound similar, except the previously reported
bird-chirping sample improved a little. The earlier mapping identifies that sample as
sample_01, outdoor prompt, seed 42. This does not establish removal of birds. No new
numerical ratings were supplied or inferred. The assistant inspected file data and
metadata, not independently identified sound events by listening.

All six adapter outputs have higher RMS than their paired base outputs, despite equal
sample peaks. The comparison was not loudness-matched or blinded. Details are saved in
outputs/lora-prompts-29974/paired_analysis.json. One listener and three seeds per prompt
support only an informal pilot conclusion: training and adapter inference work, with
mostly similar perceived quality and one reported modest improvement. Broad quality
superiority, generalization and novelty remain unproven.

Next experiment: connect the real denoiser to temporal masks, initially using the dog
adapter only within a specified interval and the base outside it. Compare against the
