This suggests prompt choice is worth controlling before evaluating adaptation. It does
not demonstrate statistical superiority or establish that isolated prompting eliminates
all extraneous sounds. Three seeds and one listener are a small informal pilot; clarity
ratings are at the ceiling. A dog whine is a within-category variation, distinct from birds.

generally, and rate those separately.
